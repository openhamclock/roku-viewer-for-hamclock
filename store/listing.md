## App name
HamClock

## On-device description (228/300 characters)
See and control your own HamClock amateur radio display on your TV. Enter its IP address once, then use the remote to move a cursor and tap anywhere on the screen. Requires a HamClock running on your home network (not included).

## Online description (1145/1500 characters)
HamClock for Roku puts your HamClock amateur radio display on your TV and lets you control it with the Roku remote.

HamClock is free software that shows a ham radio dashboard: clock, maps, space weather, propagation, satellite passes and more. This channel connects to a HamClock that you run yourself on your home network, shows its screen full size, and lets you tap anywhere on it using the arrow keys and OK.

HOW IT WORKS
- Enter your HamClock's IP address once (for example 192.168.1.20). The channel remembers it.
- The picture refreshes about once a second.
- Arrow keys move the cursor, OK taps, Play/Pause pauses the picture, and * (Options) or Rewind changes the address.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A HamClock running on the same network as your Roku. HamClock is not included and is not provided by this channel.
- HamClock's live web port (8081 by default) reachable from your Roku.

PRIVACY
The channel connects only to the address you enter. It collects no personal data, has no accounts or ads, and sends nothing to anyone else.

This is an independent, unofficial channel. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author of HamClock.
